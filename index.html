Ripple Effect: The Lost Hours — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Ripple Effect: The Lost Hours (“Ripple Effect”, “the app”) is a 2D time-travel action RPG for Android made by Kozo Technology. This policy explains what information the app and its advertising partner handle, and the choices you have.

Contact: kozo.technology@gmail.com

Information the app stores on your device

The app keeps your game progress and settings on your device only:

  • your story progress, the levels you finished, your choices, quests, small deeds, Lost Hours and achievements
  • your character: level, skills, gear, relics, materials, shards, companions and wardrobe
  • sound, vibration, graphics, controls and difficulty settings, and when the last ad between levels was shown

The app has no accounts and does not send any of this information to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and Settings → Erase the save removes your progress at any time. If you turned on Android's device backup, Android may include this data in your own Google backup. Shards are earned by playing and cannot be bought with money; the app has no in-app purchases.

Advertising and your consent

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a stated reward (a revive, a free potion, double XP or shards, a bonus chest or a forge reroll), and an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a level (never in your first two levels, and at least two minutes apart). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating-system information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent management platform. Personalised ads are shown only if you consent; otherwise you may see limited, non-personalised ads, or no ads.
  • United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Change your choice at any time: you can change or withdraw consent at any time in the app's Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings.

The game stays fully playable whatever you choose: the whole story can be finished without watching an ad, and if ads are not allowed the offers to watch one are simply not shown.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads and the consent form; the game itself works offline. Vibration is used for haptic feedback and can be turned off in Settings. The other permissions listed on Google Play (network state, advertising ID and ad services, keeping the device awake, background work) are added by Google's ads SDK. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
